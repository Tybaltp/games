Ponte del Diavolo

Islands of four tiles, bridges over the water.
The game runs in the host's browser. If the host drops out, the other player takes over after a few seconds; the clocks stop while a player is away. Clock: 20 min per player, +10 s after each move; run out of time and you lose.
Resume code

Last resort if the connection cannot be restored: either player can paste this code into “Resume a game” on the home page.

Rules
  • On your turn, place two tiles of your colour, or one bridge.
  • Island: exactly 4 orthogonally connected tiles. Fewer than 4: a sandbank. Never more than 4.
  • An island may not touch any other tile of its colour, not even diagonally. Different colours never block each other.
  • Bridge: between two of your tiles, two squares apart (straight or diagonal) or a knight's move apart. The squares it spans must be water.
  • A tile carries at most one bridge. Bridges never cross. Nothing may be placed under a bridge.
  • Opening: the first player places two light tiles; the second player chooses a colour. Dark then moves.
  • End: if Dark passes, the game ends at once. If Light passes, Dark plays one last turn, then the game ends. You may only pass when no tile can be placed; a player with no move at all must pass.
  • Each group of islands linked by bridges (sandbanks included as links) scores:
    1 island: 12 : 33 : 64 : 105 : 156 : 21
  • Tie-break: most islands, then most bridges.
