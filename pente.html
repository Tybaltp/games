Moves

    Rules
    • Five or more stones in a row wins.
    • Bracketing exactly two opposing stones (X O O X) captures them. Playing into a bracketed position is safe.
    • Capturing five pairs wins.
    • Swap2: the first player places 2 black stones and 1 white. The second player takes Black, takes White, or adds 1 black and 1 white and lets the first player choose. White moves next.
    • Fischer clock: 20 minutes each, 10 seconds added after every action. Running out of time loses the game.
