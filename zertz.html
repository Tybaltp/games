Moves

    Rules in brief
    • Marbles are shared by both players. If a capture is possible, it is mandatory.
    • Place: put a marble from the pool on an empty ring, then remove a free ring (empty, on the edge, able to slide off the board).
    • Capture: a marble jumps over a neighbour onto the empty ring just behind it; the capture continues as long as possible.
    • Isolate: a separated group of rings that is fully occupied goes to the player who isolated it.
    • Win: 3 of each colour, 4 white, 5 grey or 6 black. Also on time or by resignation.
    • Clock: 20 min + 10 s per move. It starts when both players have clicked Start the game, and stops while a player is disconnected.
